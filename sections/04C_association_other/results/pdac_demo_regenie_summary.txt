How to read this table.
  A vs B  the whole pruning decision. The sets differ in size and in
          balance, so read neff, not the headline counts. If B is no more
          inflated than A, the retained relatedness cost nothing and the
          pruning removed information for no statistical gain.
  B vs C  the mixed model, on the same people. C lower means the offset
          absorbed real structure; C higher means it absorbed signal.
  D       REGENIE without the offset. B vs D isolates the software from
          the method; D vs C isolates the offset.
  P_causal is the simulated ABO variant. Watch it alongside lambda: an
          analysis that lowers inflation by discarding real signal is not
          an improvement.
