5. GENOME BUILD
   Nothing in a .bim file records the build. It has to be established
   from the manifest and then written down. A GRCh37 dataset analysed as
   GRCh38 produces coordinates that are wrong by megabases in places, and
   the analysis will run without complaint. Everything in this guide is
   GRCh38, with variant identifiers in chr:pos:ref:alt form.
