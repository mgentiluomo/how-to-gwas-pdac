HOW TO READ THIS
  Large P values in sections 1 and 2 mean the outcome carries no detectable
  ancestry structure, at the group level or along the components. Inflation
  of the Cox scan then cannot be confounding by ancestry, and components
  added to the model remove nothing; compare 04_permutation_null.R.
